Frequently asked questions

Is AREDS2 for everyone?

No. AREDS2 is generally discussed for people with specific AMD stages. Ask your eye-care professional whether it applies to you.

Does AREDS2 restore vision?

No. AREDS2 should not be described as a vision-restoration product.

Why do zinc levels matter?

Some products use the original 80 mg zinc approach while others use lower-zinc formulas. Discuss the best fit with a clinician.
