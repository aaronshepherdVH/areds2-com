FAQ

Is AREDS2 for everyone?

No. It is generally discussed for people with specific AMD stages. Ask your eye-care professional.

Does AREDS2 restore vision?

No. The evidence-based discussion is about reducing risk of progression in appropriate patients, not restoring lost vision.
