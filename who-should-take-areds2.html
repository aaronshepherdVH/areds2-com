Patient guidance

Who should take AREDS2?

AREDS2 is generally discussed for people with intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye. The right decision depends on diagnosis, eye exam findings, medical history, and clinician guidance.

Ask first

Do not use this page to self-diagnose AMD. Ask your ophthalmologist, optometrist, or retina specialist whether AREDS2 applies to you.

AMD stage matters

AREDS2 is not normally framed as a general eye vitamin for everyone. It is best understood in the context of age-related macular degeneration staging and risk of progression.

People without AMD, or with very early findings, may receive different advice than people with intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye.

Use this site for education only. A clinician should confirm whether AREDS2 is appropriate for your situation.

Is AREDS2 a replacement for eye exams?

No. Regular eye care remains important for AMD monitoring and treatment planning.
