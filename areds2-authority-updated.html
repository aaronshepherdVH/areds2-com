AREDS2 basics, without the sales pitch

Most visitors arrive with one question: “Should I be taking AREDS2?” The honest answer depends on AMD stage, smoking history, current supplements, and your eye doctor's recommendation.

1

What AREDS2 is

AREDS2 is a specific high-dose vitamin and mineral formula studied for reducing progression risk in certain people with age-related macular degeneration.

See the formula →
2

Who it was studied in

The NEI AREDS2 trial included people with intermediate AMD in both eyes or intermediate AMD in one eye and advanced AMD in the other eye.

Patient fit

Who should discuss AREDS2 with an eye doctor?

  • People diagnosed with intermediate AMD.
  • People with advanced AMD in one eye and AMD risk in the other eye.
  • People with a smoking history who want a beta-carotene-free formula.
  • Anyone already taking high-dose zinc, vitamin E, or multiple overlapping supplements.
Important: AREDS2 is not a general multivitamin and is not proven to prevent AMD in people without AMD or early AMD.
Decision framework

What matters when comparing products?

  • Ingredient match: vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, copper, lutein, and zeaxanthin.
  • No beta-carotene: especially relevant for current or former smokers.
  • Zinc level: original 80 mg vs lower 25 mg formulations.
  • Tolerability: zinc can be hard on digestion for some people.
  • Convenience: one-capsule daily options may improve consistency.

Why zinc level deserves its own section

The original AREDS formula used 80 mg zinc. AREDS2 also evaluated lower zinc at 25 mg. The NEI reports no significant change in effectiveness when zinc was reduced, which is why both original-dose and lower-zinc products exist.

FAQ

Common AREDS2 questions

Expanding this section into 50+ questions is one of the strongest SEO opportunities for AREDS2.com.

Can AREDS2 improve vision?+
AREDS2 is not intended to restore lost vision. It was studied to reduce the risk of progression to advanced AMD in certain patients.
Can everyone take AREDS2?+
No. AREDS2 is a high-dose supplement formula studied in people with specific AMD stages. Ask an eye care professional before starting it.
Why was beta-carotene removed?+
Beta-carotene was associated with increased lung cancer risk in smokers and former smokers. AREDS2 replaced it with lutein and zeaxanthin.
Is 25 mg zinc enough?+
AREDS2 evaluated lower zinc at 25 mg, and NEI reports no significant change in effectiveness when the zinc dose was lowered. Some shoppers prefer lower zinc for tolerability.
Which product should I choose?+
Choose based on your doctor's advice and your priorities. Viteyes fits lower-zinc preference. Generic4Me fits value shoppers who want original-dose zinc.
