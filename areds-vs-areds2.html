AREDS vs AREDS2

The original AREDS formula and the later AREDS2 formula share many ingredients, but AREDS2 replaced beta-carotene with lutein and zeaxanthin.

Original AREDSAREDS2
Included beta-caroteneUses lutein and zeaxanthin
Older study formulaUpdated study formula

Why the change?

Researchers evaluated lutein and zeaxanthin as alternatives to beta-carotene. The updated approach is often preferred when discussing smokers and former smokers.
