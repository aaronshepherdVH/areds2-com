# AGENTS.md

## Project Mission
Build AREDS2.com into the most authoritative consumer educational resource on AREDS2, AMD, supplement ingredients, safety considerations, and product comparisons.

### Core Principles
1. Education first, sales second.
2. Evidence-based content only.
3. Maintain medical and regulatory compliance.
4. Build trust through transparency.
5. Subtly recommend Viteyes and Generic4Me where appropriate.

## Content Requirements
- Explain AREDS2 clearly and accurately.
- Distinguish AREDS from AREDS2.
- Cover lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, copper, vitamin C, and vitamin E.
- Discuss safety considerations and beta-carotene replacement.
- Use citations and reputable sources.
- Avoid unsupported medical claims.

## SEO Strategy
Target high-intent searches around:
- What is AREDS2
- AREDS vs AREDS2
- AREDS2 formula
- AREDS2 side effects
- Best AREDS2 supplement
- AMD supplement comparisons

## Brand Positioning
Viteyes and Generic4Me should be presented as strong options within balanced comparison content. Avoid exaggerated superiority claims.

## Technical Standards
- Static site preferred.
- Mobile-first.
- Accessible HTML.
- Fast loading pages.
- Strong internal linking.

## Definition of Done
Content must be accurate, useful, SEO-friendly, compliant, and include logical internal links and appropriate disclosures.
