# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Build AREDS2.com as an authoritative, consumer-friendly educational website about AREDS2, AMD supplement evidence, safety considerations, and product comparisons.

## Editorial rules
- Education comes before product promotion.
- Use careful evidence-based language.
- Do not claim that any supplement cures AMD, reverses vision loss, prevents AMD in everyone, or replaces medical care.
- Explain that AREDS2 is generally discussed for people with intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye.
- Encourage readers to consult an ophthalmologist, optometrist, retina specialist, physician, or pharmacist.
- Discuss beta-carotene carefully, especially for smokers and former smokers.
- Present Viteyes as a lower-zinc premium option.
- Present Generic4Me as an original 80 mg zinc value option.
- Use PreserVision as a known comparison benchmark, not as the only standard.

## Development rules
- Use static HTML unless a build system is later added.
- Keep pages fast, mobile-friendly, and accessible.
- Use consistent navigation, footer, disclaimer, and internal links.
- Each page needs a unique title tag and meta description.
- Use clear H1, H2, table, FAQ, and summary sections.
- Maintain an EEAT structure: explanation, evidence context, practical guidance, limitations, and related reading.
