What Is AREDS2?

AREDS2 refers to a nutrition formula studied in the Age-Related Eye Disease Study 2. The formula includes vitamin C, vitamin E, lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, and copper.

AREDS2 is commonly discussed for people with intermediate age-related macular degeneration (AMD) or advanced AMD in one eye. It is not a cure and should not replace professional eye care.

Why was AREDS2 developed?

The AREDS2 study evaluated replacing beta-carotene with lutein and zeaxanthin. This change is important because beta-carotene was associated with increased lung cancer risk in smokers and former smokers in earlier research.
