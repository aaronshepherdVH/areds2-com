AREDS2 basics

What is AREDS2?

AREDS2 is a studied eye-health supplement formula often discussed for people with certain stages of age-related macular degeneration. It is an educational topic to review with an eye-care professional, not a do-it-yourself diagnosis or treatment plan.

Quick summary

Typical AREDS2-style formulas include vitamin C, vitamin E, lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, and copper. The updated formula removed beta-carotene and uses lutein and zeaxanthin instead.

Why AREDS2 matters

The AREDS2 formula is important because it gives patients and clinicians a more specific framework for discussing nutritional supplementation in AMD. It is not a general promise of better vision, and it should not be presented as a cure.

Good AREDS2 education should explain what the formula is, who it may apply to, what it does not do, and what questions a patient should ask before choosing a product.

Core ingredients

Lutein and zeaxanthin

These carotenoids replaced beta-carotene in AREDS2-style formulas and are central to the modern formula discussion.

Zinc and copper

Zinc dose varies by product. Copper is commonly included to help balance high-dose zinc intake.

Vitamin C and vitamin E

These antioxidant vitamins are part of the studied formula framework and should be evaluated by label dose.

FAQ

Is AREDS2 the same as a regular eye vitamin?

No. AREDS2 refers to a specific studied formula used in a specific AMD context. A general eye vitamin may not match the AREDS2 formula.

Why was beta-carotene removed?

AREDS2-style formulas use lutein and zeaxanthin instead of beta-carotene, which is especially important to discuss for smokers and former smokers.

Should I start AREDS2 on my own?

Use this site for education, then ask an eye-care professional whether AREDS2 fits your diagnosis, medications, and health history.
