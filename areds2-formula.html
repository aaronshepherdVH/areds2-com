Ingredient guide

The AREDS2 formula, explained.

AREDS2-style products are usually built around six key ingredients: vitamin C, vitamin E, lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, and copper. Understanding each ingredient makes supplement comparisons clearer and less dependent on marketing claims.

Important

Serving size and ingredient dose can vary by brand. Always compare the current Supplement Facts label and ask your clinician whether the formula fits your diagnosis.

Core ingredients

The AREDS2 formula is not simply an eye multivitamin. It is a study-based ingredient framework that shoppers can use when comparing products. The main ingredients to look for are lutein, zeaxanthin, vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, and copper.

These ingredients matter because many eye-health products use similar language but different formulas. A front label may say eye health, macular support, or antioxidant support, but the Supplement Facts panel is where you confirm what the product actually contains.

Lutein

A carotenoid used in AREDS2-style formulas instead of beta-carotene.

Lutein and zeaxanthin

Lutein and zeaxanthin are the carotenoids that define the modern AREDS2 discussion. They replaced beta-carotene from the original AREDS formula. This distinction is especially important for smokers and former smokers to review with an eye-care professional.

When comparing products, confirm that both lutein and zeaxanthin appear on the label. Some products may include only one carotenoid or may use eye-health language without matching the full AREDS2-style ingredient pattern.

Comparison tip

Do not assume the word “eye” on a bottle means AREDS2. Look specifically for lutein and zeaxanthin.

Zinc and copper

Zinc is one of the most important differences among AREDS2-style products. Some formulas use the original 80 mg zinc approach, while others use lower-zinc versions. This can affect how shoppers compare Viteyes, Generic4Me, PreserVision, and other options.

Copper is commonly included because high-dose zinc can affect copper status. If a product contains zinc but does not clearly list copper, that is a label detail worth reviewing carefully.

Site positioning

Viteyes is positioned here as a lower-zinc premium option. Generic4Me is positioned as an original 80 mg zinc value option.

Vitamin C and vitamin E

Vitamin C and vitamin E are antioxidant vitamins included in the AREDS and AREDS2 formula framework. They are familiar nutrients, but that does not mean more is always better or that every person should add overlapping supplements.

Many adults already take multivitamins or other products containing vitamin C or vitamin E. Before adding AREDS2, compare the total daily intake from all supplements and ask a clinician or pharmacist if you are unsure.

FAQ

Do all AREDS2 supplements have the same dose?

No. Products can differ by zinc level, serving size, capsule count, and label details.

Why does copper appear on the label?

Copper is commonly included because high-dose zinc can affect copper status.

Why are lutein and zeaxanthin important?

They are the carotenoids used in modern AREDS2-style formulas instead of beta-carotene.

Should I compare cost per bottle or cost per day?

Cost per day is more useful because serving sizes and capsule counts vary.
