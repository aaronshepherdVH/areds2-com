Ingredient guide

The AREDS2 formula, explained.

AREDS2-style products are usually built around six key ingredients: vitamin C, vitamin E, lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, and copper.

Important

Serving size and ingredient dose can vary by brand. Always compare the Supplement Facts label.

Core ingredients

Lutein

A carotenoid used in AREDS2-style formulas instead of beta-carotene.

Zeaxanthin

Often paired with lutein in modern AREDS2 formulations.

Zinc

Commonly available in original 80 mg zinc options and lower-zinc alternatives.

Copper

Included in many formulas to balance high-dose zinc intake.

Vitamin C

An antioxidant vitamin included in the AREDS2 framework.

Vitamin E

Another antioxidant vitamin included in the studied formula.

FAQ

Do all AREDS2 supplements have the same dose?

No. Products can differ by zinc level, serving size, capsule count, and label details.

Why does copper appear on the label?

Copper is commonly included because high-dose zinc can affect copper status.
